AML analysts at GCC risk-ops teams spend hours per alert stitching transactions, KYC and policy into a defensible finding. Our copilot runs the whole flow inside Snowflake, driven by CoCo CLI: signal -> evidence -> documented finding -> SAR. Detection is deterministic (SQL rules + a Snowpark temporal graph search for layering cycles); the LLM never decides what is fraud. A Cortex Agent answers plain-English questions over a semantic view and Cortex Search, citing transaction IDs and policy clauses. Every SAR is machine-checked: each cited transaction must exist and belong to the case. Decisions are append-only by grant; auditors see PII masked, even in AI answers. Three CoCo skills ($aml-detect, $aml-investigate, $sar-draft) plus a Streamlit command centre. On seeded data, every scheme caught but one; 30/30 governance checks pass.
