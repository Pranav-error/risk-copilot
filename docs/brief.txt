AML analysts spend hours per alert pulling transactions, KYC records and policy together by hand. Our copilot does it inside Snowflake, run from CoCo CLI with three skills: aml-detect, aml-investigate and sar-draft.

Detection is plain SQL plus a Snowpark search for money moving in loops, so the model never decides what is fraud. It gathers evidence, quotes policy through Cortex Search, and drafts a SAR only after a named analyst escalates. Every transaction the SAR cites is checked against the ledger.

Auditors use a Streamlit app with a Cortex Agent and see only masked customer names. On held-out data, four of five rules caught every planted case.
