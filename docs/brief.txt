AML analysts at GCC risk-ops teams spend hours per alert stitching transactions, KYC and policy text into a defensible finding. Our copilot runs the whole flow inside Snowflake, driven by CoCo CLI: signal -> evidence -> documented finding -> SAR draft. Detection is deterministic and explainable (SQL rules for structuring, velocity, FATF geo-risk; a Snowpark temporal graph search for layering/round-trip cycles). The LLM never decides what is fraud. Cortex Search cites the exact policy clause and FinCEN/FATF guidance; every claim cites a transaction ID. Analyst decisions go to an append-only findings log; masking policies hide PII from auditors. Three CoCo skills ($aml-detect, $aml-investigate, $sar-draft) make it modular. Measured on seeded synthetic data with hidden ground truth: per-rule precision and recall reported in-app.
